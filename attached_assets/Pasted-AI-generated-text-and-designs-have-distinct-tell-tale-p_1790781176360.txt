AI-generated text and designs have distinct "tell-tale" patterns. Making a website feel human comes down to two main efforts: eliminating sterile AI design patterns and scrubbing generic, robotic copywriting.
1. Eliminate the AI Copywriting Triggers
AI writing relies on overused transition words, inflated corporate jargon, and balanced structural templates. Removing these gives your copy an authentic human voice.
 * Purge the AI buzzword vocabulary. Search your copy and delete or replace the following overused terms:
   * Delve, delve into, unravel, tapestry, beacon, testament, game-changer, seamless, bespoke, elevate, crucial, paramount, cutting-edge, paramount, look no further.
 * Kill robotic transition phrases.
   * Remove phrases like "In today's fast-paced digital world," "Furthermore," "Moreover," "In conclusion," and "It is important to note that."
 * Remove "AI Symmetry." AI loves lists of three where every point has equal weight, identical word counts, and starts with a bold title followed by a colon. Break this rhythm with varied sentence lengths, informal commentary, and uneven list sizes.
 * Inject opinion, experience, and specific anecdotes. AI writes in neutral generalizations. Include personal insights, specific dates, real stories, or exact numbers (e.g., state "It took us 4 months of testing" instead of "After extensive testing").
 * Use active voice and natural phrasing. Write the way you speak to a peer or client. Use contractions (don't, we've, it's) and direct statements rather than passive descriptions.
2. Fix the AI Visual & Design Clues
AI website generators and low-effort visual tools follow repetitive layout patterns.
 * Replace generic stock images and standard 3D/vector illustrations.
   * Remove glowing blue line network graphs, generic corporate handshakes, abstract floating neon shapes, and stylized 3D isometric icons.
   * Use authentic photos of real people, behind-the-scenes work, original product shots, or simple, high-quality custom graphics.
 * Avoid "Hero Section Cliché."
   * Common AI template layout: centered headline \rightarrow subheadline containing "Elevate your..." \rightarrow two pill-shaped buttons (Primary solid color, Secondary outline) \rightarrow floating glassmorphism card preview below.
   * Vary your layout, align content left, or start directly with a distinct hero media or direct call-out.
 * Limit uniform border glow and heavy dark-mode glassmorphism. AI design tools heavily over-index on dark slate backgrounds with glowing gradient borders around grid cards.
 * Fix repetitive grid patterns. AI layouts rely on 3-column feature grids where every box has an icon on top, a short title, and a two-sentence description. Mix up your content layouts with asymmetric cards, split-screen text-and-image rows, or editorial inline text.
3. Add Human Trust Signals
Adding specific details that AI cannot invent instantly builds credibility and makes your site feel genuine.
 * Include detailed bios and real team photos. Add an About page with personal background, real headshots, and authentic stories about why the project/business exists.
 * Feature specific, verifiable social proof. Replace generic testimonials ("Great service! - John D.") with real names, titles, company logos, photos, or embedded tweets and reviews.
 * Add clear contact details. Include a working email address, location/timezone info, physical address (if applicable), or direct links to active personal social accounts.
 * Add an unscripted blog or updates section. Include small personal notes, recent build logs, changelogs, or opinions on industry trends written in your natural voice.
Checklist: Quick Sweep to Run on Your Site
| Feature to Audit | Action Required |
|---|---|
| Headline Copy | Rewrite if it starts with "Unleash," "Elevate," "Welcome to," or "Transform your..." |
| Testimonials | Ensure every quote names a real person with concrete specifics rather than general praise. |
| Stock Visuals | Delete standard abstract renders or generic stock photos; replace with original assets. |
| Typography | Avoid default Inter or Roboto paired with pure #000000 text on #FFFFFF; choose custom font pairings and softened gray text (#1a1a1a). |
| Footer | Ensure real contact details, copyright year, and functioning links to real profiles exist. |
